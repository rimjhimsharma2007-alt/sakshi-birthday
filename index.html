My Baby,

Happy Birthday, my baby ❤️

Today is so incredibly special because it’s your birthday, and I am so happy that we get to celebrate your very first birthday together. Even though we are around everyone else today, my heart is completely focused on you, and I couldn't let today pass without telling you how much you mean to me. Honestly, I still don't even know how everything happened so fast.

Just 1-2 months ago, we didn't even know each other properly. And now, on your special day, you have already become the most important part of my everyday life. Sometimes I actually stop and think about it — how did a love this deep happen so quickly?

I still remember the first time I saw you.

I was standing at the entrance of SOTB, and you came in wearing that white dress, in the rain, talking to your friends. You looked like an angel walking in through the rain. Then you introduced yourself to me and everyone else. I don't know why, but I really liked the way you introduced yourself. Maybe I noticed you more than I realized at that moment.

Then we started talking about the hackathon. I love listening to you explain things. I love the way you research a topic, the way you try to understand something properly before talking about it, and the way you explain it to someone else. I love how you respect people and actually listen when someone is speaking.

And slowly, without me even realizing it, I started noticing everything about you.

The little moments.
Our time in the library.
Buddha Cafe.
The complex.
Walking around.
Talking about random things.
Just being together.

I don't think you realize how many of these small moments I keep replaying in my head, even when we are just sitting in the same room.

I especially remember the first time we were alone at Buddha Cafe. We both had our heads down on the table, and then I looked into your eyes.

Your eyes… I don't know how to explain what happens to me when I look at them. I love your eyes, your smile, your lips, your cheeks, your neck… honestly, I love everything about you. But more than how beautiful you look, I love the incredible person I see when I look at you. Celebrating the day you were born feels so natural because you are the best thing that has happened to me.

I remember that moment at the physio too. You were lying on the stretcher and I was standing near you, and for some reason I became really emotional and scared. There was this strange thought somewhere in the back of my mind — that one day, maybe we will be standing in a completely different place, in a hospital, in a pregnancy ward, with you lying there and me standing beside you.

I don't know why my mind goes so far ahead sometimes. Maybe because somewhere inside me, I have already started imagining a beautiful lifetime and a future with you.

Even when you're just travelling back and forth, I get worried. When you're sitting on the train or heading back to your room, I keep thinking about you. I wonder if you're okay, if you ate, if you're comfortable, if you're safe.

And when class ends, something in me automatically starts looking for you. My eyes search for you before I even consciously realize I'm doing it.

And anytime you're not right next to me, I miss you. I miss you a lot.

Even during short breaks or holidays, everything around me feels normal, but something huge feels missing the moment my birthday girl is not by my side.

I don't know where all of this is going. I don't know how something that started only a few days ago can already feel this deep. But having you in my life is the greatest gift, and I hope today brings you all the happiness you deserve.

I just know that I love the moments we are creating together. And I know that whenever I think about these past few weeks, I'll always remember the girl in the white dress who walked in through the rain and somehow ended up becoming my favourite person in the entire world.

Have the happiest birthday, baby. I am so glad I get to spend it with you here.

I love you.
